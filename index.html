Decapodes and the Discrete Exterior Calculus

Fluids Seminar, Mechanical & Aerospace Engineering · University of Florida · 15 October 2026

This talk introduces the Discrete Exterior Calculus (DEC), a discretization method in the growing field of structure-preserving discretization. Like finite volume methods, DEC works with integrals over the cells of a mesh. As a result, conservation laws and the identities of vector calculus hold exactly in the discrete equations rather than only in the limit of mesh refinement. We will build up DEC from the familiar operators of vector calculus and show how it applies to problems in fluid mechanics. We will also discuss Decapodes.jl, an open-source software platform for building multiphysics simulations from their governing equations. No background in differential forms is assumed.
