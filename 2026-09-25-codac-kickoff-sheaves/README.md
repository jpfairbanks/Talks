# Sheaves for Coordination Problems in Distributed Autonomous Systems

**CODAC kickoff meeting** · 25 September 2026

A 30-minute cut of the Georgia Tech DCL deck (`../2026-09-11-gt-dcl-sheaves-coordination/`)
for the center's own investigators and students. It covers cellular sheaf background,
target tracking, and the layered control architecture. It adds the nested
specification language, a slide on why the architecture applies to general
optimization rather than only to the harmonic extension, and a closing slide asking
for feedback on the layered and nested work.
