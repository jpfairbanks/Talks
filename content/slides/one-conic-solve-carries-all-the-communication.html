Write \(\mathcal{K} = S \cap C\), an affine space meeting a convex cone, and smooth it with a self-concordant barrier \(\varphi\) at level \(\mu > 0\).
The \(\mu\)-constrained harmonic extension
One Newton step — a KKT system
  • ●The sparsity survives. The Newton matrix factorizes on exactly the same machinery as \(L_A\) — so the constraint is free, structurally.
  • ●Holding \(\mu\) fixed keeps it smooth, which is what makes the reference differentiable — and the feedforward possible.
  • ●No central coordinator. This solve is the only inter-agent communication.
Constraints are enforced once, at the fleet level, and nowhere else.
