Each @system induces a graph map \(f\); push the finer sheaf forward along it, \(f_\star\mathcal{F}\). One sheaf per level: \(\mathcal{F}_N \to \cdots \to \mathcal{F}_0\).
  • ●Solve coarse, lift fine. The four-level fleet goes from 31 vertices to 8, and every team's geometry is enforced by the structure itself.
  • ●Lowering happens at compile time. Each level is a real cellular sheaf, so the layered-control results apply to it as they stand.
  • ●Endpoints are linear views. project is exact at every level. centroid averages basis coefficients above the leaves, so it is safe only on leaf teams.

The gap is the energy it costs to keep every escort ring rigid.
Proposition (Response weights). Team \(S\)'s response to each target sums to one:
Ring: 0.60 on its own target, 0.20 on each neighbour's. Hub-and-spoke: 0.74 on its own.
Teams blend their targets by design, so a deep spec can't be analysed one team at a time.
