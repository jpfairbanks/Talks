Problems and solutions are both sheaves: continuous ones over a space carry the theory, combinatorial ones over a finite structure carry the computation.
\(\mathbf{Vect}\)
Numerical computing: optimization, dynamics and control as linear algebra
\(\mathbf{Met}\)
Contractive processes: stable dynamics, convergent optimization
\(\mathbf{Sup}\)
Optimal decisions over discrete or nondeterministic search spaces
\(\mathbf{Set}\)
Feasibility: find or enumerate solutions by dynamic programming
Ordered from most structure to least. Most of our current work uses the first row.
