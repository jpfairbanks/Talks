A unified sheaf-theoretic framework for compositional, safe, stable and approximately optimal control of heterogeneous systems, and the software to use it.
THIS YEAR: THEORY
Local-to-global guarantees for time-varying, geometric and metriplectic systems
THIS YEAR: TOOLS
Conic solvers, a Rust engine, sheaves in CatColab
THIS YEAR: TRANSITION
Hardware results and an AFRL prototype
