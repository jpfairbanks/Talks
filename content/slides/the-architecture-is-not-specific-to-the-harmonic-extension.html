The composite ISS proof asks three things of the top layer. The Dirichlet energy is one program that has all three.
What the proof uses Why the harmonic extension has it What else has it
A smooth reference. \(\mathbf{p} \mapsto \mathbf{q}^\star_\mu\) is \(C^2\) with bounded sensitivity \(\sigma_\mu\) Strongly convex energy (\(L_A \succ 0\)), and a barrier held at fixed \(\mu\) Any strongly convex conic program, barrier-smoothed. For example, homological programs with convex \(f_i, U_e\)
An acyclic cascade. No agent state enters the solve Targets are oblivious. The solve reads only \(\mathbf{p}\) Any planner driven only by exogenous data. A planner that reads agent state, such as MPC closed on the vehicles, needs a small-gain condition instead.
A cheap, distributed solve, with exact derivatives for the feedforward The Newton KKT matrix has the sheaf's sparsity. It is factored along the elimination tree, and \(\dot{\mathbf{q}}^\star, \ddot{\mathbf{q}}^\star\) cost two more right-hand sides Any program whose KKT system is sparse along a graph. For example, composed objectives \(\sum_i f_i(P_i x)\), which are the ADMM and proximal problems
Swap the energy for another objective with these three properties, and the tracking certificate and the distributed solver carry over.
